Play analysis

42 goals reached on 30 October, re-measured with a search of up to a million boards.

Conclusions

  • The fewest moves explains how hard a goal felt as well as the difficulty score does. Distance explains almost nothing.
  • Extra moves grow with the fewest moves: about half a move on easy goals, 6 on hard ones (median).
  • Counting choices as blocks in the red block's chain (7–15 a move) predicts extra moves best. The game counted slide destinations (45–77) and predicted worst. Next.
  • The old stars graded the generator, not the player: most stars were never on offer. Stars now come from the fewest moves, 1 to 5. Changed.
  • With the 50k cap, the game never knew the fewest moves of a hard goal. Being tuned.
  • The first move after each page load was missing from the log. Fixed.
