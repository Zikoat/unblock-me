    Decisions: the Judge's way of counting choices

    How a person works it out: push red towards the target; whatever is in the way must move first, either way along its axis, by the least that frees the cells. A way is out at the edge, a wall, or a block already in the chain (a cycle). A decision (◆) is a block with two ways still in. Both get explored: a dead end costs thinking too.
