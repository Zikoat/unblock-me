How it reads

  • Every move takes the same width of the track. Moves used are dark, moves left are light, so the dark fill grows to the right.
  • A line across the track marks the exact point where a star is lost: finish before it and you keep the star on it. The medal has no line: its part of the track has its own colour.
  • The icons sit on the lines, on their own layer, so their width never shifts the track.
  • The rosette sits at 13, the lower bound: no solution is shorter, so finishing there is a perfect solve (and beats the game's own 15).
  • One star every 5 moves (a third of 15). Passed icons turn gray.
  • The track ends at 31. The last star sits outside it: it is never lost.
  • After an undo the medal is gray and faint, with an undo arrow under it: it can no longer be won on this goal.
