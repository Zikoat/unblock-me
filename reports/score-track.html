How it reads

  • Every move takes the same width of a track, and every track has the same total width. Moves used are dark, moves left are light, so the dark fill grows to the right.
  • A line across the track marks the exact point where a star is lost: finish before it and you keep the star on it. The medal has no line: its part of the track has its own colour.
  • The icons sit on the lines, on their own layer, so their width never shifts the track.
  • 5 stars is the game's own best, 15. The rosette sits one move before it, at 14: finishing there beats the game. Before it the track is gray.
  • One star every 5 moves (a third of 15). Passed icons turn gray.
  • The track ends at 35, a step past the last star you can lose, with the star that is never lost on its right end. Past 35 the track stays full.
  • The medal only shows once it is won: on many goals it is out of reach. After an undo an arrow under the gray part says it can no longer be won.
