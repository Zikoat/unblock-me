How it reads

  • Every move takes the same width of the track. Moves used are dark, moves left are light, so the dark fill grows to the right.
  • A line across the track marks the exact point where something is lost: finish before it and you keep the icon on it.
  • The icons sit on the lines, on their own layer, so their width never shifts the track.
  • 🏅 sits just before 15, the shortest solution the game has, at the start of the gray stripes: beating it beats the game.
  • One star every 5 moves (a third of 15). Passed icons turn gray.
  • The track ends at 31. The last star sits outside it: it is never lost.
  • After an undo the medal is an outline: it can no longer be won on this goal.
