# Cutscenes (your ideas)

An action story line told in 3D cutscenes made of blocks, with the red block as the action hero. Each cutscene's goal layout fits the scene: the level played around it looks like where the story happens.

## 1. The party

- **Setting:** outside a high-class party.
- **The meeting:** a male red block and a female gold block. She approaches him.
- **The fight:** blue brutes surround them, and a fistfight follows.
- **The escape:** they run off a balcony or rooftop onto a flying helicopter.

## 2. The race

- **The start:** a car race between several blocks, with a female block waving the flag between them.
- **The race:** they race, and engine blocks turn.
- **The end:** an opponent crashes, and the red block wins.

## 3. The train

"Train top right": only a title so far.

## 4. The deathbed

**Before the level**
- **In bed:** the red block, grey and old, lies on his deathbed.
- **His family:** his blue and yellow children are with him. One yellow daughter is pregnant.
- **The heart monitor:** it looks like the goal's stars, with him lying on his last star.
- **His wife:** an old grey-yellow block enters the room, hunched and slow. She goes to the bed and grieves beside him.
- Fade to gameplay.

**After the level**
- **The doctor:** the doctor enters. Only the yellow wife sits beside red now. The doctor glances at the heart monitor, which shows 0 stars.
- **The wife:** he checks on her with a stethoscope connected to a handheld heart monitor, and finds she has died too.
- **The soul:** as he turns to walk away, red's soul leaves his body, to the doctor's shock. It rises, then dives down into the ground.
- **The journey:** the fabric of space comes apart as the soul flies under the ground, in a sequence like the stargate in 2001: A Space Odyssey, flying between two maps.
- **The birth:** the soul finds the pregnant yellow daughter. A baby develops from cells, and then cries.
- The new red block plays on into the next level.
