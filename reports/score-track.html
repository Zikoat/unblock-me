How it reads

  • One cell per move, all the same width. Moves used are light, moves left are dark, so the light fill grows to the right.
  • A gap marks the exact point where something is lost: finish before it and you keep the icon above it.
  • The icons sit on their own layer above the gaps, so their width never shifts the cells.
  • 🏅 sits just before 15, the shortest solution the game has: beating it beats the game. The hatched cells (13 to 15) are where the real fewest could be.
  • One star every 5 moves (a third of 15). Passed icons turn gray.
  • The track ends at 31; past that the fill stays full (1 star).
  • After an undo the medal is an outline: it can no longer be won on this goal.
